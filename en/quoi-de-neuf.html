News · Changelog

What's new
in Stateri?

The changes in the app, from the most recent to the oldest. Last updated: November 18, 2026.

Stateri remains an app whose financial data stays on your device. This page only lists what is already available.

November 2026

Account security · Nov 14

Access to your account record (e-mail address and status) is now verified by a Google token. Your financial data was never stored at Stateri anyway.

September 2026

Richer voice entry · Sep 16

Dictate an expense: a local engine understands the amount, the date and the merchant on your device. An external AI connector is offered as an option, off by default, with your own key.

Fixed expense on the transaction · Sep 13

A fixed expense is now declared on the transaction itself, no longer on the category, with confirmation and undo.

Bank account identity · Sep 11

Bank icons and colours everywhere in the app; direct appearance selector in the Settings; more readable consistency streak.

"Canopy" theme · Sep 5

A new plant-inspired theme in the shop, contrasts validated against the WCAG AA accessibility rules.

More reliable scanner · Sep 5

The subscription scanner now only keeps charges of strictly identical amounts: in a test, the old widened pass produced three false detections out of four.

Subscription scanner · Sep 3

Spots recurring charges, price increases and stopped subscriptions, on your device.

Export of the filtered selection · Sep 3

Export to CSV or Excel only the transactions you have filtered.

Comparison with previous years · Sep 2

The Statistics screen compares the current year with Y-1 and Y-2.

App usable offline · Sep 1

Fix for a defect that prevented opening without a connection.

Quick valuation of an asset · Sep 1

Record an investment's value for the day from its card.
