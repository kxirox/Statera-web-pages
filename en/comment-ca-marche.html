Installation

Install Stateri
on your phone

Stateri is a PWA (Progressive Web App) — it installs straight from your browser, without going through the App Store or Play Store. Lightweight, always up to date.

  • Open app.stateri.fr in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone)
  • Tap "Add to Home Screen" in the browser menu
  • The Stateri icon appears on your phone like a native app
  • Also works on a computer through the browser
No App Store, no manual updates. The app updates itself every time you open it. Detailed installation guide →
2
Google Drive connection

Connect your
Google Drive

On first launch, Stateri asks for permission to access your Google Drive. That is where all your data will be stored — only with you, never sent to Stateri.

  • Tap "Sign in with Google"
  • Allow Stateri to create a file on your Drive
  • A stateri.json file is created in your personal Drive
  • You can view, copy or delete it at any time
Stateri accesses only the file it creates — not your other Drive files. Access is limited and can be revoked from your Google account at any time.
3
Setup

Add your accounts
and you're off

Enter your bank accounts (bank name, type, current balance) and choose your main currency. Stateri creates an opening-balance transaction for each one — you're ready to start entering.

  • Add as many accounts as you like: checking, savings, passbook, equity plan…
  • Multi-currency: EUR, CHF, and many more
  • Import your past transactions via CSV if you want history
  • Your first expense can be entered in under 10 seconds
No need to fill everything in from the start. Begin with a single account and add the others over time — Stateri adapts to your pace.
