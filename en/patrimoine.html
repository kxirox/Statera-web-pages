The metric that matters

XIRR: the only true measure of profitability

The classic annualized return assumes you invested all your capital at once. In practice, that is never the case.

The XIRR (Extended Internal Rate of Return) takes into account the exact date and amount of each payment — which makes it particularly accurate for investors who practise DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging): regular purchases, month after month.

Result: an annualized rate of return that truly reflects the performance of your scheduled investing strategy.
