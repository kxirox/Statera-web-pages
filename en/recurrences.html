Frequency

Monthly, every two months, quarterly — you decide

Not all your debits have the same frequency. Set the number of months between each occurrence and the day of the month it triggers.

  • Every month — for rent, subscriptions, salary
  • Every 2 or 3 months — for quarterly subscriptions
  • Every 6 or 12 months — for annual insurance, taxes
  • Configurable start date to match reality
Each recurring item has a start date — handy for subscriptions started mid-year or loans with a precise first due date.
Synergy

Your recurring items automatically feed the forecast

Recurring expenses and Stateri's forecast budget work together. A recorded recurring item appears automatically in the month's forecast — you see its impact on your future balance right away.

Zero double entry.

You set up your recurring items once. Every month, they slip automatically into the forecast budget as "Certain" lines. Your projection is ready before you have even opened the app.
