Overview

Stateri is a progressive web app (PWA) for personal budget management available at app.stateri.fr.

Stateri is developed and maintained by an independent developer (hereinafter "we" or "the publisher").

2

Data collected

Only one piece of personal data is collected: your e-mail address.

It is recorded when you sign in with your Google account, in order to manage your account status. The publisher has access to none of your financial information, nor to any of your transactions.

Stateri processes two categories of data, strictly separated:

  • Account data — your e-mail address, your first and last use dates and an account status (free, founder or premium). It is hosted by Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Details in section 3.
  • Financial data — transactions, accounts, categories, investments, budgets. It is never sent to the publisher.

The financial data you enter in Stateri is stored exclusively:

  • Locally on your device, in the browser's local storage (localStorage).
  • On your own Google Drive if you turn on backup (optional).

It never passes through our servers: the publisher has no financial database.

3

Account and e-mail address

Stateri does not ask you to create an account or choose a password. When you sign in with your Google account, that account's e-mail address is recorded in a Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) database.

What is recorded:

  • Your e-mail address.
  • The date of first use and the date of last use.
  • An account status: free, founder or premium.
  • Google's usual technical logs when exchanging with the database (IP address, device type), in accordance with Google's policy.

What it is used for: only to manage your account status, to measure usage globally and, where applicable, to contact you about the service (incident, important information about your account). Your e-mail address is not used for any advertising purpose and is not passed to any third party for commercial purposes. It is used for the newsletter described below only if you asked for it.

Legal basis (GDPR): performance of a contract, that is, providing the service you requested.

Newsletter (optional)

In the app, on the Sign-in & backup screen, a box that is unchecked by default lets you receive news about Stateri from time to time (new features, improvements) at the e-mail address of your Google account. Signing in with Google never subscribes you: only this box counts.

  • Legal basis: your consent (Article 6.1.a of the GDPR), given by checking the box.
  • What is recorded: your choice, the date of that choice and the version of the text displayed at that time, in the same record as the one described above.
  • Sending: the e-mails are sent by an e-mail delivery provider that acts on behalf of Stateri and cannot use your address for any other purpose. Each e-mail contains an unsubscribe link.
  • You can withdraw your consent whenever you like: by unchecking the box in the app, through the unsubscribe link in each e-mail, or by writing to contact@stateri.fr. Withdrawing is as easy as subscribing and has no effect on your use of Stateri.
  • Duration: until you withdraw your consent. After a withdrawal, only the record of your choice is kept (3 years at most), to prove that your request was respected.

No financial data, no data from your Google Drive and no information about your usage is used for this newsletter.

No financial data is attached to your account on the server side.

This database only contains your e-mail address, your usage dates and your status. Your transactions stay on your device and on your Google Drive.

4

Google Drive (optional backup)

If you choose to turn on Google Drive backup, Stateri will ask for your permission to access your Google Drive through Google's OAuth 2.0 protocol.

The permissions requested are: drive.file (access limited to the files created by Stateri only), as well as openid, email and profile (to identify the signed-in Google account).

In that case:

  • Stateri creates and reads only the backup files it created itself (a dedicated folder on your Drive).
  • Backup files are named stateri-YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM.json.
  • Stateri accesses no other file on your Drive.
  • The access token (refresh token) is stored locally on your device only.
  • To obtain and renew the authorization, a small service hosted at Vercel exchanges the authorization code with Google; it keeps neither the tokens nor the data.
  • The publisher never has access to your Google Drive or to your backup files.

Stateri's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This information is neither sold, nor used for advertising, nor used to train artificial intelligence models.

Google's privacy policy applies to the use of Google Drive: policies.google.com/privacy →

5

Contact form

The form on the Contact page is processed by Formspree, a third-party service that routes messages to the publisher.

  • What is sent: the category chosen, the content of your message and, if you provide it, your e-mail address — optional, only so that we can reply to you.
  • Formspree keeps these messages on its own servers, in accordance with its privacy policy.
  • These messages are used for no purpose other than handling your request.

If you prefer not to use this form, you can write directly to contact@stateri.fr.

6

Technical data

The app.stateri.fr app is hosted at Vercel; the stateri.fr showcase site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, these providers may collect standard technical data (IP address, browser, pages visited) in their server logs, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

The publisher has no access to these logs on a named basis and does not use them for identification or profiling.

7

Cookies and trackers

Stateri uses no tracking cookies, no advertising pixels and no Google Analytics. The app only uses Vercel Analytics, which measures traffic anonymously, with no financial data.

The only storage used by the app is technical and necessary for it to work: your local data (localStorage) and, if you turn on backup, the Google authorization tokens kept on your device.

No advertising, no profiling

No advertising cookies. No analysis of your financial behaviour. No resale of data.

8

Processors and sharing

No personal data is sold, rented or handed over to third parties, for any reason, and no data is used for advertising or profiling purposes.

Stateri relies on a small number of technical providers (processors within the meaning of the GDPR), strictly to make the service work:

  • Google — Firebase (Cloud Firestore): hosting of the account record (e-mail address, usage dates, status).
  • Google — Drive API: only if you turn on backup; the files stay on your own Drive.
  • Vercel: hosting of the app, secure exchange of the Google authorization code and anonymized audience measurement (Vercel Analytics).
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of the showcase site.
  • Formspree: routing of the contact form messages.
  • An AI provider of your choice: only if you yourself turn on AI voice entry with your own API key (off by default). This key stays on your device and is never included in the Drive backup.

"You can't sell what you don't have."

Stateri's offline-first architecture means the publisher physically has no access to your financial data: it never leaves your device and your Google Drive.
9

Retention period

  • E-mail address, usage dates, status and newsletter choice — kept as long as your account record exists. Deleting it, on simple request, deletes all of this data.
  • Financial data — under your sole control: it disappears when you clear your browser's local storage and delete the backup files from your Google Drive.
  • Contact form messages — kept for the time needed to handle your request.
10

Security

Sign-in is handled by Google and exchanges go over HTTPS: the publisher neither stores nor sees any password.

The security of your financial data depends on the security of your device and of your Google account. We recommend:

  • Using a device protected by a code or a password.
  • Protecting your Google account with two-step verification.
  • Turning on Google Drive backup so as not to lose your data if you change device.
11

User rights (GDPR)

You have a right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability over the personal data concerning you.

  • E-mail address, usage dates, status and newsletter choice — you can access it, ask for it to be corrected or deleted at any time by writing to contact@stateri.fr (reply within 30 days). Deletion is permanent.
  • Financial data — since the publisher has no access to it, you exercise these rights yourself: clear your browser's local storage and delete the backup files from your Google Drive.

You can also withdraw the authorization to access your Google Drive at any time from your Google account security settings →

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority: cnil.fr →
