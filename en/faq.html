Privacy & data
Yes. Stateri has no server that stores your financial data. All your transactions, balances, budgets and investments are written directly to a file on your own Google Drive. Stateri itself has no access to it — only your Google account can read that file.
No. Stateri asks for a restricted permission: it can only create and modify the files it created itself (stateri-YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM.json). Your other Drive files, photos, documents and e-mails are completely out of the app's reach.
Simply delete the stateri-YYYY-MM-DD-HH-MM.json files from your Google Drive and revoke the app's access in your Google account settings. Your data disappears entirely — there is nothing to delete on Stateri's side because nothing is stored there.
None. Stateri contains no tracking tool, no advertising, no behavioural analytics. The app has no interest in monetizing your spending habits — your budget never leaves your phone and your Drive.
How it works
Yes. Stateri is a PWA (Progressive Web App) that works entirely offline once installed. You can add expenses, check your stats and browse your history without a network. Syncing with Google Drive starts automatically the next time you are connected.
Yes. Since all your data is on your Google Drive, you can access Stateri from your phone, your tablet and your computer. Install the app on each device and sign in with the same Google account — your data will be synchronized through your Drive.
No. Stateri never connects to your bank. You enter your expenses manually (or by voice), or you import a CSV file exported from your online banking. Your bank credentials stay strictly between you and your bank.
Open app.stateri.fr in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), then use the browser menu to "Add to Home Screen". The app installs like a native app, without going through the App Store or the Play Store. See our detailed installation guide →
Features
Yes. Stateri supports multiple currencies natively — ideal if you have accounts in euros and Swiss francs, for example. Each account has its own currency, and Stateri converts amounts into your main display currency using the exchange rates you set. Learn more about multi-currency →
Export a CSV or Excel file from your online banking, then import it into Stateri from the Transactions tab. Stateri is compatible with the Crédit Mutuel and Revolut formats, and any standard CSV. Auto-categorization recognizes common merchants automatically. See the import guide →
Not at all. Open the Forecast tab, add your expected expenses and income for the month, and Stateri instantly calculates your projected end-of-month balance. You can set a certainty level (Certain, Probable, Uncertain) for each line. Discover the forecast budget →
Yes. The Investments tab lets you enter your purchases per account (equity plan, brokerage, life insurance, crypto…), track total performance in euros and in %, and calculate your XIRR (actual internal rate of return). Your investments are included in the calculation of your total net worth. Discover net worth tracking →
Stateri handles shared accounts through a system of contribution rates: you define the percentage of a joint account's expenses that really belongs to you. A "My share" toggle in the statistics gives you a personalized view of your budget, even on shared accounts. Discover shared accounts →
Pricing
Yes, all the features available today are freely accessible. Forecast budget, net worth tracking, multi-currency, CSV import — nothing is hidden behind a paywall. Stateri is a constantly evolving project: new features may appear in the future, possibly as advanced options.
Stateri is an independent project built with passion. Core features are free; there is no advertising and your data is never resold. The business model may evolve over time, and any change would be announced in advance on this site.
