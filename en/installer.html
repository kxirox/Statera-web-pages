Install Stateri
on your phone

Stateri installs straight from your browser in 30 seconds — no App Store and no Play Store. Only an e-mail address is needed to create your account. Just follow the steps below for your phone.

App address
https://app.stateri.fr/
You can also use Stateri directly in your browser
No installation required — just open app.stateri.fr on any device.
Open →

Android

With Google Chrome
1

Open Chrome

Launch the Google Chrome app on your Android smartphone.

2

Go to the app

In the address bar, type or paste: app.stateri.fr and press Enter.

3

Install the app

An "Add to Home Screen" banner appears automatically at the bottom of the screen. Tap it.

If the banner doesn't appear, open the ⋮ menu (three dots at the top right) and choose "Install app".

4

Confirm the installation

A dialog appears. Tap "Install" to confirm.

5

You're set!

The Stateri icon appears on your home screen. Tap it to launch the app.

Stateri works like a real app: full screen, no Chrome navigation bar, and even offline.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. Stateri stores no financial data on its own servers. All your data is saved directly to your Google Drive account, which belongs entirely to you.

Do I have to create a Stateri account?

Yes. An account is created with a simple e-mail address — it is the only personal data Stateri keeps. Your financial data stays on your device and your Google Drive.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Stateri is offline-first: once installed, it works entirely without a connection. Syncing with Google Drive happens automatically when a connection is available.

Can I use it on several devices?

Yes. Install Stateri on as many devices as you like. Your data syncs automatically through your shared Google Drive.

Why use Safari on iPhone and not Chrome?

Apple imposes restrictions on iOS: only Safari has access to the API needed to install web apps on the home screen. Chrome and Firefox on iOS cannot offer this feature.
