A budget app
that doesn't keep your data.

Stateri is a free personal budget app whose financial data stays on your device. Stateri neither receives nor stores your transactions, balances or investments: they are never sent to Stateri.

You can also back up to your own Google Drive, with a permission limited to the files created by Stateri only.

Transparency

What leaves your device, what doesn't

For the claim "your data stays with you" to be verifiable, here is the dividing line.

Stays on your device: your transactions, balances, budgets, accounts and investments.

Goes to your Google Drive, if you turn on backup: a backup file that only you can read, in your own account.

Is collected by Stateri: your e-mail address, for your account. That's all; see the privacy policy.

The trade-off, stated plainly. Since your transactions go through no Stateri server, Stateri cannot connect to your bank on its own. You import a file or type things in. If you want automatic sync, see the comparison.
Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most

Which budget app keeps my data on my phone?

Stateri: transactions, balances, budgets and investments stay on your device and are never sent to Stateri: Stateri neither receives nor stores them. The optional backup goes to your own Google Drive.

Is Stateri free?

Yes. All of Stateri's core features are free. The app has no ads and Stateri does not resell any data.

Do I have to create an account?

You just sign in with a Google account. Only the e-mail address is collected, for the account; financial data is not part of it. The details are in the privacy policy.

Does Stateri connect to my bank automatically?

No. There is no bank sync: you enter your transactions (including by voice) or import a CSV file, for example from Crédit Mutuel or Revolut. That is the price of making sure your bank credentials never pass through Stateri.
