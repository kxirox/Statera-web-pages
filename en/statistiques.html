Overview

Analyze your spending over the period of your choice

Choose from 4 preset periods — 7 days, 1 month, 1 year, or the whole history. The change in your balance is displayed instantly with the amount and the percentage change.

  • Balance change in euros and in percent, over the chosen period
  • Opening and closing balance of the period always visible
  • Filters by bank, account type and month
  • "My actual expenses" view to isolate your share of shared expenses
The "Rolling 30 days" button is a flagship feature of Stateri: instead of analyzing the current calendar month (1st to 31st), it calculates your spending over the exact last 30 days from today. Ideal for an always up-to-date view of your spending habits.

Rolling 30 days

A single button to instantly see how your spending has evolved over the last 30 days — regardless of the calendar.

Charts

Your spending by category, visualized

Scrolling down in the Statistics tab, you reach two complementary charts: the histogram of the last 12 months and the pie chart of spending breakdown over the period.

Histogram — rolling 12 months

Each bar represents a month, stacked by spending category. At a glance, you identify your busiest months and the categories that weigh most in your budget.

  • 12 months of history always displayed
  • Each colour corresponds to a category
  • Interactive legend below the chart

Pie chart — Spending breakdown

The pie chart shows the breakdown of your spending over the selected period. Each slice corresponds to a category, with the amount displayed directly on the chart.

  • Amounts displayed in euros on each slice
  • Monthly total calculated automatically
  • Tap a category to go straight to the detail in the history
Tip: clicking a slice of the pie chart or a bar of the histogram takes you straight to the History tab, filtered on that category and that period — you can then view and edit each expense individually.

The history opens filtered on your category

Here, a click on the Going out, bars, restaurants category in the chart directly opens the list of all the matching expenses — with the detail of each transaction: date, bank, account, note. Each line can be edited or deleted in one tap. A "← Back to Stats" button takes you instantly back to the charts.

Granularity

Spending by subcategory

If you have created subcategories for your expenses, Stateri lets you dive even deeper into the detail. Select a category and see the breakdown of its subcategories.

  • Select any category from the drop-down menu
  • The pie chart updates instantly with its subcategories
  • Expenses without a subcategory are grouped under "(No subcategory)"
  • Exact amounts displayed for each subcategory
Subcategories are created directly when you enter an expense. For example, the Shopping category can contain Home, Tech, Clothing, etc.
Evolution

Follow the evolution of your balance day by day

The balance evolution curve shows you precisely how your money moves over the analyzed period — with the rises (incoming income) and the falls (spending) clearly visible.

  • Day-by-day curve over the selected period
  • Balance peaks and troughs immediately identifiable
  • Precise dates on the horizontal axis
Top spending categories

Just below the curve, Stateri lists your top spending categories for the period, ranked by decreasing amount — to see at a glance where your money goes.

The curve and the category ranking update automatically when you change the period (7 days, 1 month, 1 year, all) or turn on the rolling 30 days mode.
