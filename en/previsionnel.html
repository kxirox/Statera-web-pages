Projection

Your end-of-month balance, calculated now

Enter your expected expenses and income for the month — Stateri instantly calculates your projected end-of-month balance. You know in advance whether you will be comfortable or tight.

  • Current balance updated in real time from your transactions
  • End-of-month projection based on all your planned lines
  • Gap clearly displayed between now and the end of the month
  • Filterable by account or by bank
Forecast lines do not affect your real history — they are only used to simulate and anticipate. Nothing is recorded until you confirm it.
Certainty levels

Plan according to what you really know

Each forecast line has a certainty level. This lets you tell guaranteed expenses from those that are still uncertain — and understand how reliable your projection is.

Certain

Guaranteed expense

A rent payment, a direct debit, a subscription. You know precisely that this expense will happen and for how much.

Probable

Probable expense

A planned restaurant outing, estimated groceries, a purchase under consideration. The expense is planned but the amount or date may still vary.

Uncertain

Hypothetical scenario

A purchase you're thinking about, a possible repair. You want to test the impact on your budget without including it in the main projection.

Stateri can filter the projection by certainty level — to see your balance in the worst case (everything included) or the best case (only the certain ones), depending on how you prefer to anticipate.
Threshold alert

Set the minimum balance not to go below

Set an alert threshold — for example €200 — and Stateri visually highlights if your projection falls below that amount. You immediately spot the months at risk.

  • Threshold configurable in euros, per account or global
  • Visual alert if the projection falls below the threshold
  • Ideal for maintaining a safety cushion
  • Can be combined with your overall monthly budget
Combine the threshold alert with pre-recorded recurring expenses for a fully automatic projection at the start of each month.
Conversion

Turn a forecast into a real expense in one tap

When a planned expense happens, convert it directly from the forecast. It moves into your real history — with no re-entry.

From plan to reality, with no re-entry

Your forecast lines serve as a template. Once the expense is made, a simple button moves it into your real history with all its information already filled in.

  • Convert a selected line
  • Convert all the filtered lines at once
  • Choice of target account at the time of conversion
  • The line disappears from the forecast once converted
